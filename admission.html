Admission process (PUC)
Science


PCMB

PCMB is a combination of science subjects comprises of Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics and Biology, which are taken in the first year of the Pre-University course. Students after successful completion of the PCMB Course in the second year PUC can choose either Engineering, Medical, or professional degree courses related to PCMB subjects. There are various competitive exam options after completion of the PCMB course, which include JEE (Engineering), NEET (Medical/Dental), BITSAT, COMED-K, NDA, and other state levels as well as national level entrance examinations.
Students who have passed in 10th standard with good scoring and are interested in PCMB subjects can enroll in our college. The PCMB combination also lets students opt for Biotechnology, Bioscience, Nautical Science, or other science and technology courses. The college also provides an integrated PCMB course for the students to prepare them with JEE and NEET entrance examinations to write the exam with confidence and come out in flying colours.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Students who have passed 10th/SSLC/or any other equivalent examination recognized by the PU Board Department are eligible for the admission.
  • PCMB course duration is two years

PCMC

This combination is chosen by students who have an interest in both the physical sciences and computer science. It provides a strong foundation in the core sciences (Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics) along with a focus on computer science principles and programming. Physics: The study of matter, energy, and the fundamental forces that govern the physical world. Chemistry: The study of the composition, structure, properties, and changes of matter. Mathematics: The application of mathematical principles and techniques to solve problems in various scientific disciplines. Computer Science: The study of algorithms, programming languages, software development, computer architecture, and the theoretical foundations of computation. Students opting for a PCMC combination often have a diverse range of career options available to them. This combination is particularly beneficial for those interested in pursuing careers in fields such as computer science, information technology, software development, engineering, and related areas where a strong foundation in both physical sciences and computer science is valuable.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Students who have passed 10th/SSLC/or any other equivalent examination recognized by the PU Board Department are eligible for the admission.
  • PCMC course duration is two years

CEBA

The CEBA course comprising of Computer Science, Economics, Business Studies and Accounting is a pre-university course becoming very popular among students. With a combination of commerce subjects and computers, the course offers better options for higher studies with good career prospects. Students are equipped with basic understanding of computer and its applications, this along with account management and economics.
Here is an overview of the courses offered in the CEBA stream: Computer Science: These subjects cover topic such as programming languages, data structures, algorithms, computer networks, and database management systems. It prepares students for careers in software development, IT, and other technology-related fields. Economics: These subject covers micro and macroeconomic principles and their applications in various areas of business and society. It helps students understand the economic factors that influence business decisions and the broader economy. Business Studies: This subject covers various aspects of business, such as management, marketing, finance, and human resources. It helps students develop the skills and knowledge necessary to start and manage a successful business. Accountancy: This subject covers the principles and practices of accounting, including financial accounting, cost accounting, and management accounting. It helps students develop the skills needed to manage financial records, analyze financial statements, and make informed business decisions.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Students who have passed 10th/SSLC/or any other equivalent examination recognized by the PU Board Department are eligible for the admission.
  • CEBA course duration is two years

BBA

BBA Courses are a perfect blend of theoretical learning and practical experiences. BCOM


BCom full form is Bachelor of Commerce. BCom is a 3-year UG degree program offered by universities and colleges worldwide. BCom course typically focuses on Commerce, Accounting, Economics, Finance, Management, Marketing, and other related subjects. Candidates who wish to pursue a career in the Accounting and Commerce field can enroll for BCom course. According to a survey report, with nearly 9.33 lakh enrolment, BCom has been the third most desired course in India after Class 12. Students can pursue BCom programmes such as full-time BCom, Distance BCom, or Online BCom.

BCom course provides students with a comprehensive understanding of various aspects of the business world, preparing them for careers in Accounting, Finance, Marketing, Management, and Economics. Besides BCom General or B Com Honours, several universities offer integrated Bachelor of Commerce courses such as BCom LLB, BCom MBA, and BCom CMA. Once graduated with a BCom course, candidates can opt for job profiles such as Financial Analysts, Accountants, Tax Consultants, Accounts Managers etc. The average salary that a BCom course graduate can earn ranges between INR 1 LPA and INR 9 LPA.

BA


BA full form is Bachelor of Arts. It is one of the sought-after courses in India. BA is an arts degree that can be completed in 3 years. The candidates who have completed Class 12 can pursue a BA in any specialization such as English, Hindi, History, Journalism and Psychology. BA (Hons) or BA General course can be pursued in a full-time, part-time, correspondence or distance mode of education. Candidates can seek admission to BA programmes either based on merit or based on entrance exam scores. Some popular entrance exams through which students can seek admission to BA courses are CUET, IPU CET and PUBDET.

BA graduates can work as Executive Associates, HR Managers, Copywriters, Marketing Managers, Sales Directors and in several other similar profiles. Many candidates prefer pursuing an MA or MBA after completing a BA. BA graduates can find job opportunities in the government as well as the private sector. Some of the top recruiters of BA graduates are Aaj Tak, Dainik Jagran, Milap and Goonj. The average salary range of a BA graduate in India is between INR 3 to INR 20 LPA, depending on the job profile, organization and BA specialization.

B.Sc


B.Sc full form is Bachelor of Science, which is a 3 or 4-year undergraduate program (duration depends on the specialization), structured to offer students with specific knowledge in the subjects related to Science. These BSc degrees offer students a diverse collection of academic interests within the field of Science and related disciplines. They are offered in more than 114 specializations including BSc Biology, BSc Chemistry, BSc Physics, BSc Mathematics, BSc Computer Science, BSc Agriculture, and BSc IT.

The eligibility criteria to get admission to a BSc course may differ depending on college requirements, the basic criteria are to pass Class 12 in the Science stream with either PCB, PCM, or PCMB as core subjects and a minimum 50% aggregate marks from a recognized board. Admissions are based on both merit and entrance exams. Some popular BSc entrance exams include CUET, ICAR AIEEA, CG PAT, KCET, MHT CET, AP EAMCET, etc.

MBA


MBA full form is Master of Business Administration. MBA course is a 2-year postgraduate level degree program focusing on management, business, and entrepreneurial concepts. MBA course curriculum is designed in a way that there is a combination of both theoretical and practical aspects; wherein classroom sessions and seminars take care of the theoretical knowledge and internships and workshops provide candidates with industrial exposure and real-time problems making them industry-ready.

To be eligible for MBA courses and pursue it from their dream college, one must have completed their graduation in any field (Science, Commerce, Arts) with an aggregate of 50% or more. Students can pursue MBA courses in different modes including Full-time Regular MBA, Distance MBA, Online MBA, Executive MBA, and Integrated MBA and the duration varies according to the course type. Recently, many schools has also started providing short term management programs for individuals to upskill themselves, some of the popular courses include Sports Management and Management Development Programs. Admission to MBA courses happens through entrance exams, followed by a Group Discussion (GD), Written Ability Test (WAT), and Personal Interview (PI) to assess the candidates' personality.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Accepting Exams: Karnataka PGCET

MCOM


MCom course is a 2-year PG degree focused on advanced study in Commerce, Accounting, Management, and Economics. M.Com full form is Master of Commerce. and is designed to provide students with a deeper understanding of various business practices and theories. Graduates of an MCom course are well-equipped for careers in Finance, Banking, Consulting, and academic roles, and may also pursue professional certifications or further studies such as a PhD in related fields.
To be eligible for the MCom course, candidates must make sure to have passed BCom or BCom (Hons) Class 12 with a minimum of 50% scores.
