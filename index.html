Founder Message

Greetings from Indo Asian Group.

Prof.Dr.T. Ekambaram Naidu,

Founder Chairman – Managing Trustee, of the Indo Asian Academy Education Trust, Bangalore, India, heartily welcome you to the threshold of this premier Institution of higher education in Bangalore, the metropolis of the state of Karnataka in particular, and the IT capital of the world. Indo Asian Academy Degree College has received accreditation from NAAC – National Assessment and Accreditation Council, an autonomous body established by UGC – University Grants Commission, Govt. of India, that assesses and accredits institutions of higher education in the country. The programs and curricula are comprehensive and at the same time adhere to exemplary international standards We are proud of our academic challenges and we believe in every incoming student aspirant who adds to our legacy. We have a purpose-built learning and teaching environment. Our faculty members are encouraged to provide our students with the fundamentals that they will use for the rest of their careers. We strive to bring global perspective to all its programs and activities; we are committed to our vision statement i.e. “To build in each student a strong character and the will power to excel globally” Kindness, dignity and protection of all persons are at the heart of ‘cura personalis’, meaning “care for the person” which is the theme for Indo Asian Group of Institutions. I am confident that the Indo Asian Group will meet and exceed your expectations. I remember Abraham Lincoln’s quote which reads – “I am not bound to win, but I am bound to be true. I am not bound to succeed, but I am bound to live the light that I have. I must stand with anybody that stands right and stand with him while he is right, and part with him when he goes wrong”

Prof.Dr.T. Ekambaram Naidu
