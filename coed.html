Greetings from Indo Asian Group.

Dear Students

At Indo Asian Academy Degree College & PG Centre vision is passionately pursued by all of us by not only carrying out academic and administrative activities but a holistic development of every student is looked into to make every student face the dynamic environment.

We handhold every student to excel in curricular, co-curricular and extra-curricular activities to inculcate corporate work culture and entrepreneurial qualities, we organize guest lectures and industrial visits regularly.

We believe that our responsibility lies in motivating students as well nurturing them in the right environment so that they contribute significantly to tomorrow’s world.

The need of the hour is to change with time and teach what is relevant. Keeping this in mind we are committed to offer value added courses in various subjects. The students are made industry- ready by giving soft skills and life skills training.
