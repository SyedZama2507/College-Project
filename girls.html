Greetings from Indo Asian Group.

Dear Students

I am often reminded of the quote by Brigham Young “You educate a man; you educate a man. You educate a woman; you educate a generation”. In fact at Indo Asian Girls PU College and Indo Asian Women’s Degree College, we strongly believe that we will make a positive difference not only in the life of our students but in the community and society at large.

The students have a sustained record of academic excellence over the past years under Pre-University Board and Bangalore University. The college has variety of clubs and societies which train the students to discover their forte and create awareness on social issues. These include the Career Guidance Cell, The Entrepreneurial Development Cell, The Business Line club, The Soft Skills Club, Cultural Club, The Environment Club. A number of social service activities are conducted. Every year a substantial number of the students are placed in reputed MNCs and leading Indian corporate houses.
We help young women grow into confident, creative, emotionally balanced and professionally competent individual by nurturing their all-round potential through an excellent educational system that is dynamic and innovative to women from all sections of society.

Prof. Dr. Rama K MA, MPhil, PhD
