About Indo Asian Academy



Introduction

“Be an embodiment of Wisdom, Knowledge,Intelligence and Creativity.”

Indo Asian Academy Group of Institutions is located in Bangalore, Karnataka. Students can choose to pursue Degree and Diploma programs in this institute from multiple disciplines or streams. It offers PG and UG courses in a total of 5 streams. These programs are offered in full-time mode and the institute is widely known for courses such as B.A., B.Com, B.Sc., BBA, BCA, M.A., M.Com, MBA/PGDM, MCA. The institute provides education at an affordable fee which ranges between INR 250,000-286,000. It also provides good infrastructure facilities to provide a better learning environment.


Asian Academy was established in 2007 at Kalyan Nagar. In this brief period of 15 years, our institution has offered thousands of students around the globe. We offer quality and top-notch education in disciplines of Science, Commerce, and Arts. We have also have extended our stellar education programs with value-added and certification courses. This is one of the top Degree Colleges in Bangalore cordially welcomes you to the campus of the Indo Asian Academy Group of Institutions which offers a plethora of opportunities for the students, to evolve and mature gracefully.



  • The Indo Asian Academy is Recognized, by Govt. of Karnataka and approved by the Pre-University Education Board.
  • Indo Asian Academy degree and Indo Asian Academy PG is affiliated to Bangalore University and recorded by Govt. of Karnataka
